How quality is assigned

● Bronze - every generated answer. Collected automatically.

● Silver - reused by a different node via the org index. Signals the answer was useful to more than one person.

● Gold - admin-approved wiki promotion, or a 👍 in the chat UI. Human signal of correctness.

Fine-tuning with your dataset

Once you have 500+ silver+gold examples, run LoRA fine-tuning with unsloth: 

# Export your data
anthill export-training --output dataset.jsonl --quality silver

# Fine-tune (requires GPU; unsloth handles quantisation)
pip install unsloth
unsloth train \
  --model qwen3:8b \
  --data dataset.jsonl \
  --output ./myorg-adapter \
  --epochs 3

# Register with Ollama
ollama create myorg-model -f ./myorg-adapter/Modelfile

The resulting model will be specifically good at your organisation's domain and vocabulary - similar to what Perplexity did with their search Q&A pairs.
