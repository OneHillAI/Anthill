What is this?

Take a tour

Memory is what Anthill remembers from your everyday chats and tasks, so it gets smarter without you marking anything. It sits between the cache (reuses a near-identical answer) and the wiki (curated, reviewed knowledge): short, durable facts and preferences that are recalled automatically into future answers.

Personal by default. When the same fact turns up for two or more people it is auto-promoted - to your shared team if you have one, otherwise org-wide - so it's recalled for everyone. An admin can also promote by hand, and you can turn any memory into a reviewed wiki page. Delete anything you don't want kept.
