What is Needs review?

Every wiki change is first checked by an agent. Clean changes apply automatically; only the ones it flags - a possible contradiction, duplicate, broken link, or personal data - wait here. Approve writes the page (edit the text first to correct it); Reject discards it. An upload that matches a page you already have is held here with a recommendation, and nothing changes until you choose to keep the existing page, replace it, or keep both. All decisions are logged in the audit trail. {% if scope == "personal" %}This queue is your personal wiki - only you see and approve these. {% else %}Team changes are approved by the team owner on the team page; this queue is the org wiki.{% endif %}
