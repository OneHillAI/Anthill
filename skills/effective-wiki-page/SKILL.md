---
name: Effective wiki page
description: How to write a clear, reusable wiki page for the org
when_to_use: when saving an answer or research as a wiki page
---
Write the page so a teammate can reuse it months later:

1. Start with an `# H1` title naming the topic, then a one-sentence summary line.
2. State concrete decisions and facts, and attribute them to their source.
3. Keep one topic per page; link related pages with [[other-slug]].
4. End with a short "Related" section.
5. Prefer plain, direct language. No filler.
