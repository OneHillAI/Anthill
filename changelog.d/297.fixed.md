**A model download can no longer leave the "downloading" banner stuck.** When two downloads overlapped and one of them wrote its end state late, the saved flag could be put back to a model that had already finished, so the Models page kept saying it was downloading, deleting that model was refused and the automatic image-model download kept waiting. A late write could also clear the flag while a new download was running. The flag is now always written from the live state, and writes are taken one at a time (#297).
