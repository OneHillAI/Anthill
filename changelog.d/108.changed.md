**A web-search answer now shows its steps and streams.** A question that searches the web used to leave the chat empty until the whole answer was written, then show all of it at once. Now the line under the ants says what is happening ("Searching the web", "Reading 5 pages", "Writing the answer") and the answer streams word by word. On a 16 GB Mac with a 9B model and Thinking off, a short web question showed its first step after 4 seconds and its first word after 16 seconds, instead of 60 seconds of silence. With Thinking on, the first word still waits for the model to finish thinking, and the line says so for a model that can think. If the model fails or says nothing before its first word, the answer is tried again without thinking, and then from the wiki alone. A search result that carries an instruction to the assistant is left out of the answer, on both ways of answering; the streamed answer says how many were left out. A turn that searches the web never reads or writes the answer cache. While an answer is being written the server keeps answering other requests.
