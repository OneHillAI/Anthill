**You can now remove a document or a page, and get it back for 30 days.** Before, nothing you uploaded could be removed. In the Files tab each document has a Remove link, and each page has "Remove this page". Anthill first shows what goes (the document, its kept text and the pages a model made from it that nobody has checked) and what stays (pages a person approved or edited). Removed items are not used in answers and are listed under Removed with the days left, Restore and Delete now. After 30 days they are deleted. Delete takes the item out of Anthill and its search; it cannot remove copies in backups or exports, or what a model has already been trained on.
