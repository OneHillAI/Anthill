**An upload now leaves a record, and a failure says what to do.** Under **Add a document**, **Recent uploads** lists your last ten uploads to that wiki, newest first, each with its status in plain words (Reading, Added, Waiting for your review, Held for your decision, Waiting for the model to finish downloading, Timed out, Couldn't read, or Interrupted), the page link, and which model read it (or "filed as written"). It is still there after you leave the page, and a document that waited for the model to download turns into Added or Couldn't read by itself. Only you see your own list. A scanned PDF or a picture with a text-only model no longer says "Couldn't reach your AI": it says the PDF is page images, that your AI reads text only, and to add an image-reading model in Settings > Model or upload a text version. "Couldn't reach your AI" is kept for a real connection failure. The too-large, over-the-limit and try-again PDF messages now say what to click, what the limits are (200 pages, 25 MB, 10 minutes) and to split the file or try again.
