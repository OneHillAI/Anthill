**Answers can now use the words of your own documents, not only the AI's summary of them.** When you ask a question, Anthill now also looks in the text it kept from your uploaded documents and, if a passage matches, gives the model up to two passages of your original text first, labelled as the original, with the AI's summary of the same document marked as a summary that may contain errors. Before, a fact that the summary got wrong or left out could not be found: in a test with a real model, answers about a bakery handbook were right 6 times in 12 before and 12 in 12 now. The answer line shows "Documents in context" with the document and page, also after you reload. Questions that no document answers are not slowed down; a question that does match takes about half a second longer to start on a small model. Set ANTHILL_DOC_CONTEXT=0 to switch it off.
