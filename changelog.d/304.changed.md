**The model catalogue now says where each model comes from and what it needs, and the app picks your default model by what fits your machine.** Every model's licence, region and memory fit on 8 to 64 GB machines are recorded, and three wrong licence labels are corrected (Kimi K3, Nemotron 3.5 and Llama). The old quality scores had no source, so they are cleared; the app now suggests the largest model that fits comfortably in your memory (a 4B on an 8 GB Mac, a 9B on 16 GB, a 20B on 24 GB, a 30B on 32 GB, a 35B on 64 GB). The safe build names for 12 models are stored for a later update.
