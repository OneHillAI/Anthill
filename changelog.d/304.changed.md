**The model catalogue now says where each model comes from and what it needs, and no longer claims a quality score it cannot source.** Every model's licence, region and memory fit on 8 to 64 GB machines are recorded, and three wrong licence labels are corrected (Kimi K3, Nemotron 3.5 and Llama). Because no independent source for the old scores could be found, all scores are cleared, so the app now suggests the largest model that fits your machine (on an 8 GB Mac that is Granite 4.2 3B, on a 16 GB Mac Gemma 4 12B). The safe build names for 12 models are stored for a later update.
