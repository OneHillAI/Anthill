**Anthill now keeps the text it reads out of a document, and lets you open it.** When you upload a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel or HTML file, the text Anthill extracted from it, which is what your AI is given to write the page from, is now kept beside the original. In the Files tab each such file has a "text read from it" link, and the page says you can open the text Anthill read from it, so you can check what the AI actually saw. Nothing is kept for notes (the file already is the text) or for scans and images, where no text could be read. Searching this text will follow. Documents uploaded before this update have no text kept until they are uploaded again.
