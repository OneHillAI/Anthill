**Pages and documents without a record are no longer used in answers; older wikis are prepared automatically, once.** Every page and kept document now carries a record that says whether it is active and approved, and an item without a valid record is left out of answers. A wiki made before records existed is given its records the first time Anthill reads it: it makes a backup first, changes no page text, and can be undone with `anthill knowledge inventory --undo`. You are told once that it happened. If Anthill cannot prepare a wiki (for example a read-only disk), the wiki says so and tries again at the next start. A hand-damaged record is set aside and never repaired.
