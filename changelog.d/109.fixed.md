**Long wiki pages no longer quietly drop out of a local answer.** When the pages found for a question were too long for the local model's reading window, the engine threw them away without a word and the model answered from general knowledge, fast and often wrong. Anthill now sets the window itself (8,192 tokens by default, or Ollama's own larger default on a Mac with plenty of memory; `ANTHILL_NUM_CTX` to change it) and shortens the reference, least relevant page first, so the question and as much of the wiki text and your recent conversation as fits always reach the model. A bigger window uses a little more memory.
