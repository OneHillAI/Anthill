**A greeting or a thank-you is now answered without looking anything up in the wiki.** On a wiki of long pages, "hi", "thanks!" or "ok" used to pull in the three closest pages (about 1,000 tokens) before the model answered, because some page always scores above the cut-off. On a 16 GB Mac with a 9B model, in the chat with web search and Thinking off, that took 7 to 8 seconds to the first word, and the reply sometimes talked about the unrelated pages. Now it takes about 2 seconds. Only a message made entirely of phrases from a short fixed list counts: hello, hi, good morning, thanks, thank you, ok, yes, no, sure, fine, nice, how are you, bye, and a few common German, French, Spanish and Italian ones. "hello, what is our refund policy?" and "how much is it?" still look things up, and so does a "yes", "ok" or "yes thanks" that answers a question the assistant just asked.
