**Backups now include your wikis and the original files you uploaded.** A full backup saved the database, files, skills and keys, but left out every personal, project and organization wiki and the originals behind them, so restoring it on a new machine, or after a deleted data folder, brought back the settings but none of the knowledge. Backup and restore now cover all of it. Restoring also takes a safety copy of the current wikis first, so a mistaken restore can be undone. Restoring a backup made before this fix leaves your current wikis as they are.
