**A cached answer no longer outlives the pages it was built from.** Anthill reuses an earlier answer when you ask the same question again, but nothing checked whether the wiki had changed since. After you edited a page or added a document, the old answer kept coming back. Now a cached answer is dropped when a page it cited was changed, or when a page about the question was added or changed after it was made, and it is not served while a page it cited is missing. The same question asked again also replaces its old answer instead of sitting next to it. If nothing in the wiki has changed the cache is used exactly as before, and the check takes a few milliseconds even for a wiki of thousands of pages. Answers that change as you teach Anthill new things will be answered again once.
