**You can now correct a wiki page.** Open a page and choose "Edit this page" to change its text, for example to fix a wrong fact in a summary a model wrote. Your text replaces the page and the earlier text is not kept. The page then says it was edited by a person, and a model upgrade will never rewrite it. You can edit pages in your own wiki, in a team wiki if you own the team, and in the organisation wiki if you are an admin. Answers use the corrected text from the next question on.
