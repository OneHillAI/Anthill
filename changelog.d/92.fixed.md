**Tasks are easier to read in dark mode and for people with low vision.** On the Tasks pages the main button, error text, secondary text and form-field outlines were too faint against their background, mostly in the dark theme. They now meet the WCAG AA contrast levels in light and dark, a task's scope (Solo or Org) is marked by shape as well as colour, and the page colours come from shared theme settings. The primary button is slightly darker in dark mode on every page.
