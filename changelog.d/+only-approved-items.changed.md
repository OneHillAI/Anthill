**An upload that is waiting for a decision, was rejected or has been removed is no longer used in answers.** Before, the text kept from a document could still be found and used even when the page made from it was held for review or rejected. Now every route that reads your knowledge (chat, the agent, scheduled tasks, the chat widget and the cached answers) skips anything that is not approved and active. Uploads made from now on are covered. Documents uploaded before this version have no record yet, so an old upload that was waiting or rejected is still read until `anthill knowledge inventory --apply` is run; it makes a backup first, never changes a page's text, and can be undone.
