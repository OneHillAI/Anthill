**The pages before the app now look like one product.** Setup, sign in, forgot password, choose a new password, accept an invitation and confirm your email all show the Anthill hill and name at the top of one card width, with the same wording and placeholders. Every field now has a label tied to it, so screen readers announce it and clicking the label focuses the field. Sign in offers "Create an account" only where sign-up is open, and says "Your own private AI" instead of calling every install an organisation. The desktop splash uses the same font as the app, and the installer says "Installing Anthill" without the emoji or "(admin)". The four-step progress bar no longer sticks out of the sign-up card: it shows the current step's name and four numbered markers. The "Get started" card now always offers "Sign in" and "Forgot your password?".
