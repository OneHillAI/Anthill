**A page an agent proposes now waits for your review before it reaches the wiki.** Before, an agent that wanted to add or change a wiki page wrote it straight in, even though it described the page as pending review. Now the page appears in the review queue of the wiki it belongs to (yours, a project's or the organisation's), marked as written by an agent, and nothing changes until a person approves it. An agent that has no person to review its pages cannot write to the wiki.
