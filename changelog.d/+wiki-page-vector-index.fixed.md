**Questions no longer wait while Anthill re-reads your whole wiki.** Before answering, Anthill used to turn every wiki page into a search vector again for every question, so the wait before the first word grew with the size of your wiki (about 10 seconds for 60 pages in our measurement). It now keeps those vectors on your machine, next to the answer cache. It builds them in the background once the app and its embedding model are running, and adds a page's vector as soon as the page is saved, so a new question finds them ready: the same 60 pages take a few milliseconds. If the embedding model is still starting or downloading, Anthill waits for it for a few minutes before giving up, and a question that arrives earlier simply builds what is missing. Which pages Anthill picks, and what it answers, are unchanged. A wiki with no pages costs nothing.
