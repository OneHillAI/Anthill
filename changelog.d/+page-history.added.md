**See a page's earlier versions and restore one.** A page that has been changed now has a History link. It lists the page as it is now and each earlier version with its day and who wrote it; opening a version shows its text, and "Restore this version" makes it the page again as a new version, so nothing is erased. When a save from the edit screen is refused because the page changed, the screen keeps your text and links to the page as it is now and to its history.
