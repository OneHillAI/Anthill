**The desktop app opens filling the screen, then remembers its size.** The first time you open Anthill the
window fills the screen. After that it reopens the way you left it: the same size and place, maximised or not.
Nothing shows before the app is ready. On a Mac the window first appears at its usual size and then, a moment
later, fills the screen or takes its saved size, so a brief resize is expected. An earlier build of this change
was tested on the Mac app; the final version has not been re-run as a packaged app, and the Windows and Linux
builds get the same change and have not been tested yet.
