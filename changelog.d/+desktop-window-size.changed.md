**On a Mac, the desktop app opens filling the screen, then remembers its size.** The first time you open
Anthill on a Mac the window fills the screen. After that it reopens the way you left it: the same size and place,
maximised or not. Nothing shows before the app is ready. The window first appears at its usual size and then, a
moment later, fills the screen or takes its saved size, so a brief resize is expected. The window on Windows
and Linux is unchanged.
